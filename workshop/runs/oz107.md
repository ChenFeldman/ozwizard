## Answering the two customers
`scripts/recheck-scans.ts` is read-only:
```
npx tsx scripts/recheck-scans.ts --from 2026-09-01 --to 2026-09-08 --customer <id>
```
Every saved scan keeps its full dependency list. For each scan in the window, the script recomputes the verdict two ways: with the advisories published by the scan date (what a working feed would have said) and with everything known today. It flags scans whose result would differ. I tried it on a made-up store: a vulnerable scan saved as `allow` was flagged as `block`, a clean one was unchanged, and one outside the window was skipped. Saved scans don't record which customer they ran for, so pass `--customer` or it uses the global policy.
