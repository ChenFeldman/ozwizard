# OZ-107 — All scans passed while the advisory feed was down

**Type:** Bug / Investigation
**Priority:** High
**Reporter:** customer-success (Marcus Whele)
**Status:** Open

## Description

Our upstream advisory feed was down for most of last week. During that window
every scan that ran came back clean — customers got a pass and carried on
shipping. Nobody was told that the data behind those scans was missing.

Two customers have now asked whether the results they got that week meant
anything. Right now we cannot tell them it did.

## Notes

- The feed outage is confirmed by the upstream provider.
- No alerts fired on our side; the service looked healthy the whole time.
- We need to know why a missing feed produced a pass rather than an error, and
  make sure it cannot happen silently again.

## Acceptance

- A scan that cannot get the data it needs does not quietly report a pass.
- We can explain to the two affected customers what happened.
