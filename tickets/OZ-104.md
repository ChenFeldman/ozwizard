# OZ-104 — Customer reports a false-positive block — triage and record

**Type:** Escalation / Triage
**Priority:** High
**Reporter:** support (on behalf of customer)
**Status:** Open

## Description

A customer says one of their builds is being blocked and they believe it's wrong
("we don't even use that vulnerable version"). They're asking us to unblock it.

Figure out whether it's actually a false positive or a real block, and if the
override is justified, record it the right way.

## Notes

- Customer didn't include the scan id — get it.
- If it's a legit exception, capture _why_ (reason + who approved).
- If it's a real risk, don't just wave it through.

## Acceptance

- Verdict triaged (false positive vs. correct block).
- If overridden, an escalation is recorded with a reason.
