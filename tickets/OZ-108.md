# OZ-108 - Acme wants high-severity advisories to warn, just for them

**Type:** Feature
**Priority:** Medium
**Reporter:** customer-success (Dana Ruiz)
**Status:** Open

## Description

Acme is in the middle of evaluating us and is pushing back on how strict we are.
Today a high-severity advisory stops their build. They would like high to come
back as a warning instead, so their teams can see it and decide.

They were clear that this is for their account only - they are not asking us to
change it for anyone else, and they would be unhappy if we did.

## Notes

- Their account id is `acme`.
- Critical advisories must keep blocking, for Acme and for everyone else.
- This is a paid-evaluation account, so it is worth doing properly rather than
  as a one-off.

## Acceptance

- High-severity advisories return a warning on Acme's scans.
- Every other customer's verdicts are unchanged.
- Critical still blocks for Acme.
