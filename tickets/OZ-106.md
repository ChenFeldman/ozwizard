# OZ-106 — Old advisory got warn instead of block

**Type:** Bug
**Priority:** Medium
**Reporter:** customer-success (Dana Ruiz)
**Status:** Open

## Description

A customer scanned a package that has a critical advisory against it and the
scan came back `warn`, not `block`. They expected anything critical to stop the
build.

The advisory in question is not a new one — it has been published for a while.
The customer's read is that our severity thresholds are wrong somewhere, since
critical is supposed to map to block.

## Notes

- The customer quoted our own docs back at us: "critical → block".
- Newer advisories on other packages do block correctly, which is what makes
  this look like a threshold bug.

## Acceptance

- A critical advisory produces the verdict the customer expects.
